Privacy Policy — SortMySpend

This privacy policy applies to the SortMySpend app (the "Application") for mobile devices, created by Sourav Halder (the "Service Provider") as a free service. This service is provided "AS IS."

Information Collection and Use

SortMySpend is built to keep your financial data on your own device. The Application does not require you to create an account, and the Service Provider does not operate a server that stores your transactions, statements, or personal information.

When you import a bank or credit card statement (PDF), here is exactly what happens:

1. The Application reads the text content of the PDF on your device.

2. That extracted text is sent to Google's Gemini API so it can be parsed into individual transactions and automatically categorized.

3. The resulting transaction data (merchant names, amounts, dates, and categories) is saved only on your device, in the Application's local database. It is never uploaded to a server operated by the Service Provider.

Because step 2 involves sending statement text to a third-party AI service, Google may process this data according to its own privacy policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy. The Service Provider does not control, and is not responsible for, how Google processes data submitted to its API beyond generating the requested response.

The Application may also collect standard, non-identifying technical information common to all mobile apps, such as your device's operating system and general crash/error information, if and when the Service Provider adds crash-reporting tools in a future version. As of this version, no analytics or advertising SDKs are integrated into the Application.

The Application does not collect precise location data.

Your Control Over Your Data

Because all transaction data lives locally on your device:

  • You can delete all data stored by the Application at any time via Settings → Clear all data inside the app, which immediately and permanently erases every statement, transaction, and merchant record on your device.
  • Uninstalling the Application also removes all locally stored data.
  • There is no server-side copy for the Service Provider to delete on your behalf, because none exists.

Third-Party Access

The only third party the Application shares data with, as part of its core functionality, is Google (via the Gemini API), for the sole purpose of parsing and categorizing the statement text you choose to import. The Service Provider does not sell, rent, or share your information with any other third party, except:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • when the Service Provider believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

Data Retention Policy

Transaction and statement data is retained locally on your device for as long as you use the Application, until you delete it yourself (via Clear all data or uninstalling the app). The Service Provider does not retain a copy of this data, as it is never transmitted to or stored by the Service Provider.

Security

The Service Provider is committed to safeguarding your information. Because financial statement data is processed on-device and stored locally rather than on a central server, the Application's design itself limits exposure of your data to a central point of failure. The Service Provider nonetheless takes reasonable technical measures (such as using your device's secure storage APIs where applicable) to protect any sensitive configuration data the Application manages.
